About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Robotics Program at KAIST, working in the Mobile Robotics and Intelligence Laboratory (MORIN) with Prof. Jinwhan Kim.

My research is on making marine robots know where they are when GPS is gone. That means underwater SLAM, bathymetric mapping, and geomagnetic field models that stay useful even as inertial drift grows. I also work on LiDAR perception for autonomous surface vessels, including vessel re-identification in cluttered harbor scenes.

Before shifting toward underwater localization, I spent several years on LiDAR-based computer vision for vehicles and ships. I presented that line of work at OCEANS 2025 in Brest, where it received third place in the Student Poster Competition.
