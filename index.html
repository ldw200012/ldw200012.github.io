About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Robotics Program at KAIST, working in the Mobile Robotics and Intelligence Laboratory (MORIN) with Prof. Jinwhan Kim.

I work on computer vision and deep learning for robotics. For several years that meant LiDAR perception — how a vehicle or vessel understands a cluttered scene from a point cloud. My focus has since shifted to SLAM from 2.5D bathymetric data, and to mapping methods that still work underwater when GPS is gone.

I received an M.S. in Robotics from KAIST in 2024 and a B.S. in Intelligent Mobile Systems from Jacobs University Bremen (now Constructor University), where I was advised by Prof. Francesco Maurelli. In 2025 I presented LiDAR re-identification work at OCEANS in Brest (third place, Student Poster Competition) and as a late-breaking poster at IROS in Hangzhou.
