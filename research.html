Projects

Research

I work on localization and mapping for marine robots when GPS is unreliable: bathymetric SLAM, geophysical field maps, and LiDAR perception at the waterfront.

Underwater Bathymetric SLAM

Current work focuses on SLAM from 2.5D bathymetric structure. Seafloor relief is a stable spatial cue for AUVs, and I am interested in representations that stay consistent under sonar noise, sparse coverage, and long-duration drift.

Geophysical Field-based SLAM

Earth’s magnetic field varies smoothly underwater and can serve as a map when visual landmarks are scarce. I have worked on Gaussian process models of geomagnetic intensity that remain useful even when inertial navigation is drifting, including spatiotemporal mapping for AUV localization.

Maritime LiDAR Perception

Harbors and waterfronts are cluttered, reflective, and only partly observed. I develop LiDAR methods for marine vessel re-identification, including a branch-augmented network presented at OCEANS 2025, and CALM-Net, shown as a late-breaking poster at IROS 2025 and now under review at IEEE Sensors Journal.
